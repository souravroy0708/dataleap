What I’d tell you over coffee

  1. Your shift is an advantageMost interview loops for India offices run in the IST daytime. Your mornings are free: ask recruiters for 10 am to 1 pm slots and you will interview fresh, not after a night shift.
  2. Write your resume in scale, not client names“Built a CDC pipeline moving 2 TB a day with 15-minute freshness” beats “worked for a US insurer”. Recruiters search for platform, streaming, lakehouse and ownership.
  3. Level is negotiable, the habit is notCandidates moving from services are sometimes offered one level below what they target. If the work, the team and the pay are right, take it and earn the promotion. Your stories decide where you land.
  4. Ask every recruiter for the loopHow many rounds, which ones, coding language, design focus. Then prepare round by round instead of for everything.
  5. Referrals beat portalsMessage ex-colleagues who moved to product companies. A short note with your strongest story and the role link gets read; a portal application often does not.
  6. Apply in one batchOffers that land together can be compared and negotiated. Plan the notice period with your family before you sign.
