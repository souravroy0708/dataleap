From scratch to offer in 24 weeks

For a data engineer with 10+ years in services, targeting staff or senior DE roles at Uber, Google, Databricks, Visa and similar in 2027. About 10 hours a week alongside a full-time job: 75 minutes on weekdays, 2 to 3 hours on Saturday, Sunday for revision or rest.

The weekly routine

Phases

  1. Week 1Baseline
    • Two timed mediums from D1 and D4. Note where you stall.
    • One design case cold (Real-time trip and surge metrics). Record yourself.
    • Set your reminders and daily goal.

    Exit: you know your weak spots, written down as notes.

  2. Weeks 2 to 7Core patterns
    • DSA: D1 to D6, the P1 topics. Python fluency: collections, heapq, generators, sorting with keys.
    • System design: distributed fundamentals (S1) and storage formats (S4). Read the first half of Designing Data-Intensive Applications alongside.

    Exit: 70% of P1 DSA items solved on your own; you can explain partitioning, replication and Parquet layout without notes.

  3. Weeks 8 to 13Depth
    • DSA: D11 design-a-structure, D10 graphs, D8 binary search; keep revising P1.
    • System design: streaming (S3), batch and Spark (S2), modelling (S5), ingestion and CDC (S6). Practise each design case in parts.

    Exit: mediums in 25 minutes on 4 of 5 attempts; every S1 to S6 concept has a pinned summary note.

  4. Weeks 14 to 19Full designs
    • Two full design cases a week, 60 minutes each, scored against the staff signals.
    • Estimation and cost (S12), data quality (S8), orchestration (S7), serving (S9).
    • DSA stays at one a day, mostly from the revision queue.

    Exit: 10 cases done end to end; you can size and cost a design in 5 minutes.

  5. Weeks 20 to 22Mocks
    • Two mock interviews a week with peers or paid mock services: one coding, one design.
    • Company focus: read the target company's engineering blog and adjust (see Companies).

    Exit: two mocks in a row where you would hire yourself.

  6. Weeks 23 to 24Apply and interview
    • Apply in batches so onsite loops land close together.
    • Keep the daily run going between loops, revision only.
