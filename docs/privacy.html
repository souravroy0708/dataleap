Privacy

Privacy policy

DataLeap keeps everything on your device. There is no account, no server and no tracking.

What DataLeap stores, and where

Your practice sessions, notes, statuses, retrospectives, stories, mock interviews, plan, settings and the running timer are stored only on your device: in the Android app, in the app’s private database; on the website, in your browser’s storage. DataLeap has no servers and no accounts, so the developer cannot see any of it.

What DataLeap sends

Nothing. DataLeap contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries and sends no data anywhere. Links to practice sites (for example NeetCode, YouTube or your course provider) open in your browser, where those sites’ own policies apply.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders and motivation you configure.
  • Alarms and reminders / exact alarms: so reminders fire on time, including when the app is closed and after a restart.
  • Microphone (optional): only when you tap a 🎤 to speak into a text box, and only while the listening sheet is open. Audio is not recorded or stored by DataLeap; your phone’s speech service turns it into text. If you never tap a microphone, it is never used.
  • Internet: to open links and, on some phones, for the phone’s own speech service. DataLeap itself sends nothing.

Exports and backups

Backups and CSV exports are created only when you ask, saved to your device’s Downloads folder. What you do with them, such as sharing them with an AI assistant, is your choice.

Deleting your data

Use Reminders → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything.

Children

DataLeap is a career tool for adults and is not directed at children.
