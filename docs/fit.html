Eating for a 2:30 to 11:30 shift

  1. A moderate deficit, not a crashFor a 35-year-old man around 72 kg who trains, roughly 1,800 to 1,950 kcal a day usually gives 0.4 to 0.6 kg a week. Adjust by the 2-week trend, not by hunger on one day.
  2. Protein at every mealAim for about 110 to 130 g a day: dal, paneer, curd, eggs, chicken or fish, soya, and whey if you like. Protein keeps the muscle you are building while the fat comes off.
  3. The post-office danger zoneGetting home after midnight is when the extra calories happen. Eat a proper dinner at the office break, and keep one planned small snack for home: curd, fruit or a glass of milk.
  4. Roti and rice, measuredKeep them, but measure portions for two weeks so you know what you actually eat. Fill half the plate with sabzi and salad.
  5. Drinks countSugar in chai, juices and colas adds up fast. Water through the shift; chai with less sugar.
  6. Sleep is a fat-loss toolSeven hours keeps hunger hormones in check. The 12:30 bedtime in your plan matters for both goals.

General guidance, not medical advice. If you have a health condition or take medication, check with a doctor before changing diet or starting heavy training.
