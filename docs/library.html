Every lesson from your Aced (formerly Exponent) data engineering and system design courses, ranked for a staff data engineering loop. Open a lesson on aced.io, then log the time here: it counts towards today's system design.

How to use the course

  1. Data engineering first. L1 to L3 are the core: the interview loop, pipeline design and data modelling. Do every lesson.
  2. System design: concepts and craft, not consumer apps. L4 and the must-do lessons in L5 and L6 cover what a staff DE loop probes. Lessons marked "Skip unless time" are general software design (chat apps, video streaming, games).
  3. Design cases: attempt first, then open the lesson. 45 to 60 minutes on your own, score it with the rubric, then compare with the course answer.
  4. SQL is a separate round. L7 is listed for completeness; time logged there does not count towards the daily goal.

Lesson content stays on aced.io, behind your login. Keep the saved offline copies private: GitHub Pages sites are public, and those pages are paid course material that also carry your account details.
