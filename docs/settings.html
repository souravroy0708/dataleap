Reminders and habit ladder

Reminders give prep a fixed slot in a busy day. They stay quiet once that part is done, and each one carries a line to keep you going.

Make sure they reach you

1. Phone calendar (works even when DataLeap is closed). Download the reminders as a calendar file and open it on your phone. Each reminder repeats on its days with an alert and a link back here. If Android does not open it, import it at calendar.google.com, then Settings, then Import.

2. Browser notifications (while DataLeap is open in a tab). On Android, open the site in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen" so it works like an app.

A website cannot wake your phone on a schedule without a push server, so use the calendar option for reminders you cannot miss. The Android app will schedule real alarms.
