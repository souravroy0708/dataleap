# Content guidelines

1. **Links, not copies.** Store the title and a link: DSA questions link to NeetCode (`https://neetcode.io/problems/<slug>`; the slug often differs from LeetCode's, so check it on the site). System design items link to Exponent, YouTube or a blog. Never paste a problem statement, video transcript or article text.
2. **Own words for question-only items.** DE-style questions without a public link get 1 to 3 sentences written by us, plus one follow-up.
3. **DE angle.** Where a classic DSA question maps to data work, add one short line (for example Merge Intervals → sessions).
4. **Topic and priority.** Every item belongs to exactly one topic. P1 topics are what DE loops ask most; P3 are rare.
5. **Design cases** have a 1 to 2 sentence prompt with concrete scale, 3 interviewer follow-ups, and optional "fits" companies.
6. **Company tags** describe fit or public reports, never claims of an exact question asked.
7. **IDs are stable.** Item IDs come from the title slug; renaming an item changes its ID and loses its history in the prototype, so fix typos only.
