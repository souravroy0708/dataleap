**Short description (80 chars max):**
Daily system design and DSA practice to move to a product-company data role.

**Full description:**

Moving from a service company to a product company as a data engineer? You already have the experience. DataLeap gives you the daily habit to show it in the interview.

SYSTEM DESIGN FIRST
• 70+ data engineering design cases and concepts: streaming, lakehouse, CDC, Spark, data modelling, cost and staff-level judgement
• 45-minute mock interview simulator: six timed phases, follow-up questions from the interviewer, a staff-level rubric, and an export you can review with your AI assistant
• Practise each design one part at a time, then as a full round

DSA FOR DATA ENGINEERS
• 79 questions tied to real data work: dedupe, sessions, top-K, joins, watermarks, files larger than memory, starting at Easy

A COACH THAT KNOWS YOUR DAY
• Two-minute setup: your shift timings, office days, wake and sleep, and who you are doing this for
• A day plan built around your shift and your family, with reminders that fire on time and stay quiet once you are done
• A coach that speaks to you by name, tells you what skipping really costs, and protects your sleep
• Habit ladder from 10 minutes a day to interview pace

TRACK EVERYTHING
• Live timer that stops itself at 30 minutes, time reports by day and week, interview readiness score, streaks and 37 milestones
• 2-minute recall drill on your own notes (spaced repetition)
• Weekly retrospective, story bank for the project deep-dive round, mock interview log
• Applications board from referral to offer, with follow-ups and offer comparison
• Home-screen widget with your streak and today's task
• Voice input in every text box
• Export everything as CSV for your own analysis

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
