# Staff data engineering interviews: what the content targets

## DSA

- Mostly medium difficulty, Python, 25 to 30 minutes per question.
- Data-shaped problems: dedupe, group-by, sessions, intervals, top-K, merging sorted files, streaming over files larger than memory.
- Design-a-structure questions (LRU, rate limiter, key-value store with TTL) appear more at senior and staff levels.
- Google and Databricks loops are often reported as closer to software engineering coding bars: add Hard questions in P1 topics.

## System design

Staff candidates are judged less on naming tools and more on:

- Scoping: consumers, freshness SLA, correctness, scale.
- Sizing and cost.
- Correctness under failure: idempotency, exactly-once where it matters, late data, replays, backfills.
- Operability: SLOs, data quality, lineage, on-call.
- Platform thinking: multi-tenancy, contracts, ownership, migrations.

## Not covered

SQL, data modelling exercises as a separate round, and behavioural or leadership rounds. Check each target company's loop and prepare those separately.

Interview formats change by company, team and year. Read recent candidate reports for the exact role before an onsite.
